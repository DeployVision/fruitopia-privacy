Privacy policy

This policy covers our Fruitopia Basket Bash TestFlight beta with Google demo ads.

Updated November 3, 2026

Your game on your device

The game saves preferences, scores, and harvest progress on your device. We do not operate a game account or a server that receives these records. Your device may include this data in backups. The beta has no account registration or in-app purchases.

Live device motion controls the fruit basket. The game uses those readings during play without saving or transmitting them. It does not request access to your photos, contacts, microphone, camera, or location services.

Google demo ads

This beta uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK with Google's fixed sample identifiers. It requests demonstration ads, including through Settings → Show test ad. These ads do not earn revenue for Deploy Vision LLC. The SDK can contact Google when preparing an ad before you open the preview.

Google's SDK may process network and device information, including your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, crash reports, and performance data. Google describes these uses as advertising, analytics, and improving its services. What is processed depends on the SDK and device settings; sample ads still involve network requests.

This build does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. It includes Google's User Messaging Platform SDK, but the fixed demo configuration does not request publisher consent forms. This demo does not validate a future publisher advertising or consent setup.

See Google's SDK data disclosure and Google's privacy policy for its data handling, controls, and retention practices.

TestFlight and feedback

Apple collects beta crash and usage information and shares it with us. Feedback you choose to send may include comments and screenshots. Email invitations or feedback may also identify you by name or email; joining only through a public TestFlight link does not reveal your name or email to us.

We use beta diagnostics and feedback to investigate problems and improve the game. If you email us, we receive your address and the information you send so we can respond. See Apple's TestFlight privacy information for the details of Apple's collection and retention.

Keeping and removing data

Local game data stays on your device until it is replaced or you delete the app. Offloading the app may retain its data. Backups are managed separately through your Apple or device backup settings.

Leaving the beta or deleting the app does not automatically delete feedback, correspondence, or diagnostics already received by us, Apple, or Google. We keep received reports and correspondence for troubleshooting and support; there is no fixed automatic deletion period for those copies. Contact us to ask about access or deletion of information we hold. We will address the request and explain any limits, including information controlled by Apple or Google.

This policy page

This page contains no advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process request information, such as your IP address, under the GitHub privacy statement.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests, contact Deploy Vision LLC at vitali@deployvision.com. We will update this page and its date when this beta's data practices change.
